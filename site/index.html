Features

Built for the court, not the boardroom.

Everything designed around the moment you're mid-rally and need to update the score fast.

Big Tap Zones
Score with a single tap on either half of the screen. Designed for live play, even with sweaty hands.
Instant Undo
Mis-tap? Fix it instantly. One tap to go back, no dialogs, no confirmation screens.
Match History & Stats
Win rate, streaks, opponent records, and form trends. See exactly how you're improving.
Doubles Support
Track partner matches with full doubles scoring, server rotation, and partner analytics.
Shareable Result Cards
After every match, share a beautiful result card to social media or iMessage — dark and light themes.
100% Private & Offline
No account needed. No tracking. Everything stays on your device. Works anywhere on the court.
How it works

Up and running in seconds.

1
Start a match

2

Tap the big zone for each point. Undo anytime with one tap.
3
Finish the game
RallyTrack auto-detects game point and saves your match stats.
4
Share & review
Post a result card, then check your stats and history over time.
