Features

Built for the court, not the boardroom.

Everything is designed around the moment you're mid-rally and need the score updated fast.

Doubles serving, solved

RallyTrack follows the serving team, the score order, and whether you're first or second server. Lose a rally and it handles the next server or the side-out for you.

Score from your wrist

Start a match on iPhone, then score points, undo, check the server, follow the timer, or end the match from Apple Watch. Everything stays in sync.

Rivalry records

Every saved match builds a head-to-head record against that opponent. See who leads the series and carry the story into the next rematch.

Big taps, instant undo

Large tap targets you can hit without looking, and one tap to undo a mis-scored rally. No dialogs, no confirmation screens.

History & streaks

Wins, losses, final scores, streaks, recent form, opponent results, and partner results. Your match data stays on your device.

Private and offline

No account, no ads, no internet required. Scores, player names, and history stay on your iPhone.
