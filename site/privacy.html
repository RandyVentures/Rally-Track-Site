Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 14, 2026

Our Commitment

RallyTrack ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information.

All data created in RallyTrack (match history, scores, stats) is stored locally on your device only. We do not have access to this data.

Data Stored Locally

The following data is stored on your device:

  • Match scores
  • Match dates and times
  • Opponent names (if you enter them)
  • Win/loss statistics

This data never leaves your device unless you choose to export it or sync it (future feature).

Third-Party Services

RallyTrack does not currently use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

If we add such services in the future, we will update this policy and notify users.

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transfer your data to third parties because we do not collect it.

Children's Privacy

RallyTrack does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app is rated 4+ and is safe for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at:

Email: support@rallytrack.app
Website: rallytrack.app

Your Rights

Since we don't collect any data, there is nothing to delete or export. All your data exists only on your device and you have full control over it.

You can delete all your data at any time by:

  • Using the app's "Clear all data" feature (in Settings)
  • Uninstalling the app
