Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

The short version

RallyTrack doesn't collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data. Everything stays on your device. We have no servers holding your information.

What we collect

Nothing. RallyTrack does not collect any personal information. The app runs entirely on your device without sending data to external servers.

What stays on your device

All match history, scores, stats, preferences, and player names are stored exclusively in local storage on your iPhone. This data never leaves your device.

Third-party services

RallyTrack uses RevenueCat to process in-app purchases (the optional Pro upgrade). RevenueCat handles purchase validation through Apple's payment infrastructure. RallyTrack does not use any advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

In-app purchases

The optional Pro upgrade is processed entirely through Apple's App Store. RallyTrack never sees or stores your payment information.

Data deletion

You can delete all your data at any time by using the Clear All Data option in the Profile tab, or by uninstalling the app from your device.

Children's privacy

RallyTrack does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we update this privacy policy, we'll post the revised version here with an updated date. We'll only make changes that are consistent with our commitment to not collecting your data.
