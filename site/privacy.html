Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

The short version

RallyTrack keeps your match history, scores, stats, preferences, and player names on your device. We do not require an account, do not sell your data, do not run ads, and do not use your data for tracking.

What we collect

RallyTrack collects limited, privacy-preserving usage analytics so we can understand which features are being used and improve the app. These analytics may include events such as app opened, match started, match completed, share tapped, stats viewed, purchase started, purchase completed, purchase restored, review prompt shown, and notification setting changes.

Analytics events may include limited match metadata, such as match type, score values, win or loss result, match duration, and aggregate match counts, along with feature usage details such as the stats tab viewed or whether sharing completed. They do not include your full match history, player names, opponent names, notes, payment information, contact information, or precise location. They are associated with a random app-generated identifier, not your name, email address, or Apple ID.

What stays on your device

All match history, scores, stats, preferences, and player names are stored locally on your iPhone. This app data stays on your device unless you choose to share a match result using iOS sharing features.

Third-party services

RallyTrack uses RevenueCat to process in-app purchases (the optional Pro upgrade). RevenueCat handles purchase validation through Apple's payment infrastructure.

RallyTrack uses TelemetryDeck for privacy-preserving product analytics. We use these analytics for app functionality and product improvement only. We do not use analytics for advertising or tracking, and we do not share analytics with data brokers.

In-app purchases

The optional Pro upgrade is processed entirely through Apple's App Store. RallyTrack never sees or stores your payment information.

Data deletion

You can delete all your data at any time by using the Clear All Data option in the Profile tab, or by uninstalling the app from your device.

Children's privacy

RallyTrack does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we update this privacy policy, we'll post the revised version here with an updated date.
