Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Data Collection

RallyTrack is designed with privacy in mind. All your match data, scores, and statistics are stored locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers.

What We Don't Collect

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • Match scores or statistics
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics
  • Device identifiers

Local Storage

All your data is stored exclusively on your device using iOS local storage. Your match history and statistics remain private and under your control.

Third-Party Services

RallyTrack does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app functions completely offline without requiring an internet connection.

Future DUPR Integration

When DUPR integration is available, it will be entirely optional. If you choose to connect your DUPR account, only the data necessary for rating sync will be shared with DUPR's servers according to their privacy policy.

Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time by uninstalling the app or using the "Clear All Data" option in the app settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at support@rallytrack.app
