🆘 Common Questions

How do I start a new match?

Tap the "+" button on the home screen, enter your opponent's name, select singles or doubles, and tap "Start Match."

How do I score a point?

Simply tap the large score area for you or your opponent. The score will increment by 1.

I made a scoring mistake. How do I fix it?

Tap the "Undo" button at the bottom left. This will revert the last score change.

How do I end a match?

Matches automatically complete when one player reaches 11 points with a 2-point lead. You can also manually end a match by tapping "End Match."

Where is my match history?

Tap the "History" icon in the bottom navigation bar to view all your past matches.

How do I delete a match?

In the Match History screen, swipe left on any match and tap "Delete."

Can I sync my data across devices?

Not yet! iCloud sync is coming in a future update. For now, all data stays on your device.

Does RallyTrack work offline?

Yes! All features work completely offline. No internet connection required.

Why can't I find RallyTrack on Android?

RallyTrack is currently iOS-only. Android support may come in the future based on demand.

Will RallyTrack integrate with DUPR?

Yes! DUPR integration is planned for a future update. We'll announce it when it's ready.
